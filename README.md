# P4 Fertility – Homepage Redesign

## Website Overview

P4 Fertility is a UK private fertility healthcare provider offering fertility assessments, diagnostic investigations and assisted reproductive treatments, including IVF, ICSI and IUI. The clinic operates across Birmingham, London and Watford.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating P4 Fertility's personalised, consultant-led approach while improving the clarity of clinic locations, treatment pricing and patient information. It serves individuals and couples exploring fertility assessments, investigations and treatment options.

## Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Clinic Locations and Booking Information
Improved the presentation of Birmingham, London and Watford clinic locations through a clearer location structure. The new approach addresses inconsistencies between the homepage, other website sections and online booking system, helping patients identify clinic addresses, available services and relevant booking options.

### 2. Clearer and More Consistent Treatment Pricing
Reorganised the presentation of consultation fees, diagnostic costs and treatment pricing to reduce conflicting information across the website. The revised structure prioritises consistent clinic-specific prices, clearer inclusions and additional charges, and accurate pricing information.

### 3. Improved Content Quality and Clinical Trust
Refined service descriptions and patient-facing information to replace awkward wording and unclear explanations with professional, accessible language. The design direction also gives greater attention to consultant profiles, clinical credentials and verified patient testimonials rather than relying on older trust signals.

## Design Improvements

- **Visual Hierarchy:** Improved the prominence of clinic information, fertility services, pricing and consultation opportunities.
- **Content Organisation:** Structured locations, treatment categories and diagnostic services into clearer sections.
- **Location Presentation:** Made the three operating locations easier to identify and navigate.
- **Pricing Transparency:** Improved the organisation of consultation and diagnostic fees to make costs easier to understand.
- **Content Consistency:** Established clearer, more professional language across service descriptions and patient guidance.
- **Trust and Credibility:** Strengthened the presentation of consultant expertise, clinical information and patient feedback.
- **User Experience:** Created a more logical journey from exploring fertility services to finding a suitable clinic and arranging an appointment.
- **Conversion Focus:** Improved the visibility of consultation and booking opportunities.

## Project Objective

The redesigned homepage gives P4 Fertility a clearer and more consistent digital presence. It addresses three important patient-facing issues: clinic location confusion, inconsistent pricing and unclear content. The overall direction reinforces the clinic's personalised fertility care positioning while making essential information easier to understand and access.
