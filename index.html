A different perspective

Your fertility journey deserves more than a standard approach.

Fertility care involves more than choosing a treatment. It starts with understanding your individual circumstances, your medical history, your concerns and the outcomes that matter to you.

P4 Fertility focuses on detailed assessment and personalised reproductive medicine. Our consultants use available clinical information and evidence-informed approaches to guide decisions about investigations and treatment.

Whether you are exploring your fertility for the first time, considering IVF or seeking answers after previous treatment, the first step is understanding what may be appropriate for your situation.

The P4 philosophy

Four principles. One personalised approach.

P4 represents a way of thinking about fertility care that brings prediction, prevention, personalisation and patient participation together.

01

Predictive

Use available clinical information to help understand potential ovarian response, treatment outcomes and risks.

02

Preventative

03

Adapt investigations and treatment protocols to individual clinical circumstances.

04

Participatory

Encourage informed decisions through clear discussions and meaningful involvement in your care.

Helpful information

Questions to help you take your next step.

Understanding fertility treatment can take time. Here are some starting points to help you explore your options.

Ask Our Team ↗
What fertility treatments does P4 Fertility offer?
P4 Fertility publishes information about IVF, ICSI, IUI, fertility preservation, diagnostic assessments, recurrent miscarriage management, donor treatment and surrogacy consultations. Availability may vary by location and should be confirmed with the team.
How do I arrange my first fertility consultation?
You can explore available appointments through the official appointment page or contact P4 Fertility by telephone or email.
Does everyone need IVF to achieve pregnancy?
No. Different fertility circumstances may call for different investigations or treatment approaches. Your consultant can discuss which options may be appropriate following an individual assessment.
Are treatment costs the same at every location?
No. P4 Fertility publishes separate cost information for Birmingham, London and Watford. Medication, investigations and other clinical requirements can also affect the final cost.
Can I book a virtual appointment?
The official appointment information includes virtual booking options for certain consultation types. Check the appointment page for current availability and suitability.
Where can I find information about treatment risks?
Discuss the potential benefits, limitations and risks of each treatment with your fertility specialist. You can also consult the Human Fertilisation and Embryology Authority for independent information about fertility treatments and add-ons.
